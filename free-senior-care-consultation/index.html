Please note

What this consultation is, and is not.

The free consultation is an informational conversation about Adult Family Home care and Magnolia's homes. It is not a medical evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not replace advice from your loved one's physician or care team. Admission depends on a separate care assessment, the home's ability to meet your loved one's needs, and current availability; a consultation does not guarantee placement.

If your loved one is facing a medical emergency, call 911.
