How it works

What families can expect.

Communication

Respectful, unhurried communication

Caregivers use calm, simple language, give one step at a time, and allow time to respond. They learn each resident's preferences, history, and cues so that care feels familiar rather than rushed.

Daily routines

Familiar rhythms through the day

Predictable times for meals, rest, hygiene, and activities can reduce confusion and anxiety. Routines are adjusted as a resident's needs and abilities change.

Personal care

Help with everyday tasks

Caregivers provide cueing, supervision, or hands-on help with bathing, dressing, toileting, and eating, based on the resident's care plan.

Safety awareness

Watching for changes

Caregivers watch for changes in sleep, appetite, mood, walking, or behavior that may signal pain, illness, medication effects, or progression, and report them so the care plan can be reviewed.

Family collaboration

Families stay part of the picture

Families and POAs share history and preferences, hear about meaningful changes, and are included in care planning conversations.

RN-Led Clinical Excellence + Family-Centered Care

The role of RN oversight

Sosena Mekuria, RN, guides care planning so the team understands each resident's needs and knows when to involve the resident's healthcare providers. An RN is typically on site at each home every other day, and trained caregivers provide day-to-day support 24 hours a day. Magnolia does not provide 24-hour on-site nursing.

Good to know

Limits and fit

Dementia affects people very differently. Each home can only accept and continue to care for residents whose needs it can safely meet alongside the residents already living there. Some needs, such as care that requires a secured memory-care unit, may be better served in another setting. We discuss fit honestly before admission and as needs change.

Questions families ask

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Can a person with dementia live at Magnolia?

Often, yes. Whether a specific person is a good fit depends on an assessment of their needs, the home's ability to meet them safely, and room availability.

FAQ

Is a nurse on site all day?

No. Sosena Mekuria, RN, provides regular RN oversight and care planning, and trained caregivers provide support around the clock. Magnolia does not provide 24-hour on-site nursing.

FAQ

How will we know how our loved one is doing?

Magnolia keeps families and POAs informed about meaningful changes in routines, mood, health, or care needs, and is available to talk through questions.

Services are provided when appropriate for the individual resident, based on assessment, the negotiated care plan, and each home's ability to meet needs safely. This page is general information, not medical advice.
