How it works

What families can expect.

Assessment

It starts with understanding the resident

Before move-in, the home reviews the resident's assessment, abilities, routines, and preferences: what they can do on their own, where they need cueing, and where they need hands-on help.

Care plan

A negotiated care plan guides each day

In Washington, each Adult Family Home resident has a negotiated care plan developed with the resident and their representative. It spells out what help is needed, how it is given, and the resident's preferences, so every caregiver works from the same plan.

Daily help

Assistance with everyday activities

Bathing and showering, dressing, grooming and oral care, toileting and continence care, eating, walking, and transfers, including mechanical lift transfers when the care plan calls for them.

Independence

Doing with, not for

Caregivers encourage residents to do as much as they safely can, offering reminders or stand-by help before stepping in.

Changing needs

Plans are updated as needs change

When a resident's abilities change, the care plan is reviewed and updated with the resident and family. RN-guided care planning helps the team notice when a review is needed.

RN-Led Clinical Excellence + Family-Centered Care

The role of RN oversight

Sosena Mekuria, RN, guides care planning so caregivers know what help is needed and how to give it safely. An RN is typically on site at each home every other day, and trained caregivers provide day-to-day support 24 hours a day. Magnolia does not provide 24-hour on-site nursing.

Good to know

Limits and fit

Some support, such as awake overnight care, dedicated one-on-one support, or two-caregiver assistance, may be available for an additional fee, as described in each home's Disclosure of Services. Each home can only provide care it can safely deliver given the needs of residents already in the home.

Questions families ask

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

How is the level of help decided?

Through the resident's assessment and negotiated care plan, which is reviewed when needs change.

FAQ

Will my loved one be encouraged to stay independent?

Yes. Caregivers encourage residents to do what they safely can and offer cueing or stand-by help before doing tasks for them.

FAQ

Can Magnolia help with two-person transfers or overnight care?

Two-caregiver assistance, dedicated one-on-one support, and awake overnight care may be available for an additional fee. Ask during your consultation.

Services are provided when appropriate for the individual resident, based on assessment, the negotiated care plan, and each home's ability to meet needs safely. This page is general information, not medical advice.
