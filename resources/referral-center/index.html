When to refer

Refer when home no longer feels safe or sustainable.

  • Senior cannot safely return home
  • Dementia or memory care support is needed
  • Medication management is becoming difficult
  • Fall risk or supervision needs increased
  • Family caregiver burnout
  • Care needs have changed after an illness or hospital stay
  • Smaller residential care setting preferred
